## About us
- GrassCrafts sells handwoven natural-fibre products made by Indian artisans: baskets, trays, gift boxes, hampers, planters, mats and home decor.
- Website: https://grasscrafts.com
- Product links look like: https://grasscrafts.com/products/{handle}

## Shipping
- Free shipping across India on orders above ₹1,300.
- Orders below ₹1,300: ₹120 shipping charge.
- We ship only within India.
- Do not promise delivery dates. If asked, say delivery times depend on the pin code and the team can confirm.

## Payments
- Cash on delivery (COD) is currently NOT available. Customers pay online at checkout.

## Help and contact
- Contact page: https://grasscrafts.com/pages/contact
