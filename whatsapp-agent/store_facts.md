## Caring for natural-fibre products
- Our products are handmade from natural fibres (such as grass, cane, jute and other plant fibres), so small variations in size, colour and weave are normal and part of their charm.
- Keep them dry and away from direct sunlight for long periods, which can fade natural colours.
- Dust with a soft dry brush or cloth. For marks, wipe gently with a barely damp cloth and let the piece air-dry fully in shade.
- Do not soak, machine-wash or leave items in damp places, as natural fibres can lose shape or develop mould.
- Planters: use a plastic or ceramic inner pot; do not put soil or water directly in a woven basket.
