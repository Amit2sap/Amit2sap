Where GrassCrafts can win

Top competitors across eight segments, the openings they leave, new markets and export countries, and what to do in the next 30 days.

The short answer

The market leaders are mostly resellers with big catalogues and weak reviews. GrassCrafts is a maker, already the 3rd most-named brand in AI answers, but almost invisible in Google search. The fastest share gains are in jewellery and watch boxes, basket pages for Diwali, wedding and corporate searches, and wall plates and water hyacinth mats, where competitors are thin or overpriced.

Three openings competitors leave

PackagingJewellery and watch boxes

No natural-fibre maker shows up in search or AI answers, and we are already AI-cited for "handwoven jewellery boxes bulk". Box makers publish their jeweller clients (Tanishq, Kalyan, PC Jewellers), who are our target buyers.

SEOBasket searches for Diwali, weddings and corporate gifts

Held by resellers with cotton-yarn or kraft products. A maker-direct Sabai or Kauna basket page with real dimensions and a "from ₹" price can take these.

PriceWall plates and water hyacinth mats

All top wall-plate results are one seller. iTokri's water hyacinth mats cost ₹2,000+; ours are ₹250–480, with a live 500-mat corporate enquiry.
