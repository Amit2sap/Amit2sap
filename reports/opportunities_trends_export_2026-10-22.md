### 1. Corporate year-end, New Year and FY-end ESG gifting

**Why now**
- Gifting firms confirm New Year orders by about 1 Dec (Saif Gifting; see `agents/STRATEGY.md`).
- Our first website bulk enquiry was for 500 water hyacinth placemats for corporate gifting (18 Oct).
- Corporate buyers are moving to eco gifts. A 2024 GlobalData study reportedly found that 58% of corporate buyers chose eco-friendly gifts ([CSR Journal](https://thecsrjournal.in/greener-gifting-why-sustainable-choices-matter-more-than-ever/)). This is a secondary citation.
- Big buyers order at scale. Reliance Jewels ordered 8,000 gift baskets and JK Lakshmi 3,000, both from FNP (`sales/buyer_intelligence_2026-10-18.md`).
- Also in the window: Women's Day on 8 Mar (pitch in Jan) and FY-end on 31 Mar.

**Who buys:** gifting firms (Swageazy, Kavyatva, Kottanz, FNP Corporate, Advantage Club) and the HR or admin teams at companies with 200+ staff.

**What to sell**
- Kauna hamper baskets
- Sabai gift boxes
- Water hyacinth placemats in sets of 4/6
- Cane desk organisers

**How to reach them**
- The existing `corporate.json` leads.
- Paperworld India / Corporate Gifts Show (Messe Frankfurt).
- A "Sustainable corporate gifting" landing page on our site with a bulk form.

**First 30 days**
1. Send the year-end email to every corporate lead by 7 Nov.
2. Quote the 500-placemat enquiry.
3. Prepare 3 ready-made hamper bundles (₹500 / ₹1,000 / ₹2,000 tiers, filled by the buyer).
4. Send 5 sample kits to the gifting firms that reply.

**Revenue estimate:** 2–4 orders of 100–500 units at about ₹400 a unit = **₹80,000–₹8,00,000** from Nov to Mar. The ₹400 unit price is the assumption in `agents/STRATEGY.md`.

### 3. Plastic-free gift packaging for D2C brands

**Why now**
- India's plastic packaging EPR rules apply to brands of every size, with no small-business exemption for D2C brands that ship online.
- The PWM (Amendment) Rules 2026 of 31 Mar 2026 tightened certificates and audits ([Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=16444), [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/plastic-waste-management-rules-2026/print_manually)).
- ASCI requires proof behind "eco-friendly" claims. A reusable handwoven basket or box is a claim brands can prove.
- Gift boxes from beauty and Ayurveda brands now sell on Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart. Soulflower is one example ([Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/hub4business/soulflowers-thoughtful-gifting-for-every-occasion)).
- Forest Essentials runs a dedicated corporate and wedding gifting desk ([forestessentialsindia.com](https://forestessentialsindia.com/corporate-gifting)).
- Food brands already pay for baskets. SMOOR (₹2,925–3,555), Bikanervala, Gopaldhara and Theobroma all use basket hampers (`sales/buyer_intelligence_2026-10-18.md`).

**Who buys:** founders, packaging heads and gifting heads at tea, coffee, chocolate, dry-fruit, Ayurveda and clean-beauty brands.

**What to sell**
- Sabai boxes with lids
- Kauna hamper trays
- Small bamboo trays
- Water hyacinth pouches (a new small SKU)

These can be co-branded with a tag. We do not print on the grass.

**How to reach them**
- The `gourmet.json` and `wellness.json` leads.
- New targets added today: Forest Essentials.
- Instagram and D2C brand directories.

**First 30 days**
1. Make a 1-page "basket-as-packaging" brochure (sizes, fill ideas, reuse story).
2. Email 15 brands with a Valentine's (14 Feb), Holi (22 Mar 2027) and Eid al-Fitr (about 9–10 Mar 2027) angle. Brands lock this packaging in Dec–Jan.

**Revenue estimate:** 1–2 brand orders of 200–1,000 units at ₹250–450 = **₹50,000–₹4,50,000** from Jan to Mar 2027.

### 5. Export to EU basket importers (Netherlands, Germany)

**Why now**
- **Market size.** CBI says European basketry imports rose from €594m (2020) to €833m (2024), with an €1.1bn peak in 2022. About two-thirds came directly from developing countries, mostly natural materials. Europe is about 45% of world basketry imports ([CBI](https://www.cbi.eu/market-information/home-decoration-home-textiles/basketry/market-potential)).
  - A different search snippet gives €433m → €559m. Treat the size as roughly €0.6–0.8bn and confirm on the CBI page.
- **Importers by country.** Germany imported $152m of HS 4602 basketwork and France $108m in 2023 ([OEC](https://oec.world/profile/hs/basketwork)).
- **Duty.** The India–EU FTA was concluded and is expected to be signed by Dec 2026 and to take effect by Feb–Mar 2027. Handicraft tariffs of up to 10% are to go to zero on entry into force ([ELP law update](https://elplaw.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/India-EU-FTA-Update.pdf), [WION](https://www.wionews.com/india-news/india-eu-free-trade-agreement-what-landmark-deal-means-for-india-1782126858649/amp)). This is still draft text.
- **EUDR.** The EU deforestation rule excludes bamboo and rattan ([Preferred by Nature](https://www.preferredbynature.org/nl/node/10282)). Grass, jute and water hyacinth are not among the regulated commodities, but confirm the Annex I CN codes.
- **Live buy request.** A Netherlands importer wants 200–500 pieces of each type of seagrass, water hyacinth, rattan or bamboo basket, CIF, HS 460219 ([go4WorldBusiness #1329192](https://www.go4worldbusiness.com/buylead/view/1329192/wanted-:-woven-baskets.html)).

**Who buys:** importer-wholesalers and Scandinavian or Dutch brands that source from India.
- Van der Sar Import, De Lier ([IPM Essen exhibitor list](https://www.ipm-essen.de/world-trade-fair/exhibitor-list/detail/van-der-sar-import-83347))
- Van der Leeden Mandwerk, an Ambiente exhibitor ([Ambiente](https://ambiente.messefrankfurt.com/frankfurt/en/exhibitor-search.detail.html/van-der-leeden-mandwerk-bv.html))
- Madam Stoltz, Denmark, which says its craft sourcing includes India ([madamstoltz.dk](https://madamstoltz.dk/blog/find-your-boho-wholesale-inspiration-here/))

**What to sell:** a water hyacinth and Sabai/Kauna storage assortment in 3 nesting sizes, plus Madurkathi mats.

**How to reach them**
- Email and samples.
- **Ambiente Frankfurt, 29 Jan–2 Feb 2027** ([Messe Frankfurt](https://ambiente.messefrankfurt.com/content/dam/messefrankfurt-redaktion/ambiente/2027/press/press-releases/amb27-wdc-ausstellung-25-jahre-talents-lokale-perspektiven-en.pdf)). Visit, or exhibit through the EPCH India pavilion.
- **Maison&Objet Paris, 14–18 Jan 2027.** Dates are from secondary listings only.

**First 30 days**
1. Get an IEC.
2. Apply for EPCH RCMC.
3. Sristi builds an FOB Kolkata and CIF Rotterdam quote for the go4WorldBusiness request.
4. Courier 6–10 samples to 2 importers.

**Revenue estimate:** 1 trial order of 500–1,500 pieces at FOB $4–8 = **$2,000–12,000 (about ₹1.7–10 lakh)** by Q2 2027. The FOB band is our assumption and needs Sristi's costing.

### 7. Export Mithila makhana to the UAE, US and Canada

**Why now**
- **Volumes.** APEDA/Crisil dashboards show makhana exports grew from about 6,700 MT (2020) to 25,130 MT (2024).
- **Buyers.** The US takes 40%, Canada 20% and the UAE 17% ([APEDA dashboard Nov 2025](https://apeda.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-01/MIC_November_Monthly_dashboard_Makhana_311225.pdf)).
- **Narrower official figure.** Under the new makhana HS code (from Jul 2025), PIB reports 7,264.89 MT worth ₹192.96 crore in FY2025-26 ([IANS](https://ianslive.in/india-exported-over-7000-metric-tonnes-of-makhana-to-over-20-global-destinations-in-fy26--20260808151310)). The two series use different scopes, so don't add them together.
- **Government support.** A ₹476 crore Makhana scheme runs 2025-26 to 2030-31.
- **Live buy requests.** UAE buyers ask for 1–4 × 40 ft containers of suta 4/5 (`sales/data/ci_demand.json`).
- **Gulfood 2027 runs 15–19 Mar 2027** in Dubai ([Austrade](https://www.austrade.gov.au/en/events/gulfood-2027)).

**Who buys:** Indian-grocery importers and retailers.
- Krishiv Foods (US), which imported makhana in a Jun 2026 shipment ([ImportKey](https://importkey.com/i/krishiv-foods))
- Al Adil (UAE), with 50 GCC stores ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/supplements/staying-true-to-roots))
- LuLu Group India sourcing (Kochi) ([Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/supplements/lulu-continues-to-strengthen-india-gcc-trade-links-through-expanded-sourcing-network))

**What to sell:** plain Mithila makhana in retail packs (100/250 g) or 10 kg bulk. The higher-value route is a **makhana gift basket** (makhana inside a Sabai box) for Diwali and Eid in the diaspora.

**Compliance**
- APEDA RCMC and an FSSAI export licence.
- US FDA food-facility registration with a US agent. **Renewals and new registrations run 1 Oct–31 Dec 2026**, an even year ([Food Safety Works](https://foodsafetyworks.com/insights/us-fda-registration-for-food-facilities-in-india-a-gateway-to-global-markets/)).
- FDA Prior Notice for every shipment.
- UAE food registration and labelling (Federal Law 10/2015).

**Competition and margin:** competition is high on bulk makhana and margin is thin (US price about $19.5/kg). The gift basket is where we are different.

**First 30 days**
1. Confirm whether our makhana supplier already holds an FDA registration and an APEDA RCMC.
2. Price 10 kg and 250 g for the UAE CIF.
3. Reply to 2 UAE buy requests.

**Revenue estimate:** 1 pallet trial of 500–1,000 kg = **$10,000–20,000 in turnover**, but the margin is thin. The gift-basket version is the better margin. These are estimates.
