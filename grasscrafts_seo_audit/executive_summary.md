# Executive summary — what Search Console shows (30 Oct 2026, observed data only)

1. **Flat traffic.** Last 28 days 166 clicks / 9,072 impressions vs 167 / 9,357 the 28 days before; average position slipped 7.50 → 8.15. 12-month total 1,623 clicks.
2. **Non-branded search barely converts to clicks.** Last 3 months: non-branded queries 84 clicks from 13,882 impressions (0.61% CTR); branded 57 clicks from 700 (8.1%).
3. **The homepage is ranking for category searches it can't satisfy.** "straw mat" (578 impr, pos 9.7), "cane planters" (600, 13.9), "cane basket" (413, 9.5), "cane box", "jute mat(s)", "jute mat for table", "fiber storage box", "wall rugs" all land on `/` with 0–3 clicks. The matching collections are weaker or missing (e.g. /collections/cane-planter at pos 25 for "cane planters").
4. **Ranking near the top with zero clicks** (title/snippet/image problem, the fastest wins): "wedding hamper basket" pos 1.96–2.5, 252–266 impr; "basket for wedding gift" pos 3.0, 197; "wedding basket for bride/groom" pos ~2, 40 each; "basket for wedding" 2.8; "jute mat(s) for decoration" pos 3.7–3.9; "jute floor mat large" pos 4.8; "cane ottoman" pos 3.7–4.2, 173 impr. (Wedding-basket title/description already rewritten 30 Oct — re-measure 13 Nov.)
5. **Sabai grass is the biggest single query** (1,565 impr, pos 7.9, 9 clicks) and lands on the sustainability page, not the Sabai collection or buyer's guide.
6. **Madurkathi cluster splits between the blog and the collection** (madurkathi, madurkathi mat(s), madur mats: collection at pos 17–24 for "mats").
7. **Mobile is 74% of impressions** (6,680 of 9,072) at a better position (7.8) than desktop (9.3). India 65% of impressions.
8. **Product rich results work:** product snippets 2,296 impressions (2.4% CTR); merchant listings 30 impressions at 30% CTR.
