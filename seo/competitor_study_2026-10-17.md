## 2. Main competitors

| Competitor | Why it wins | Commercial levers |
|---|---|---|
| **iTokri** | About 100k long-tail product pages with origin and artisan copy. Ranks for "madur mat online". Trustpilot 4.5 (292 reviews). | **Free shipping and COD above ₹500**, free gifts, international shipping, reseller programme |
| **Brown Living** (Kadam Haat, ONEarth) | Sustainability blog covering materials and corporate gifting. Ranks for Kauna bags and "eco corporate gifts bulk". | Corporate "Sustainable Edit" with a bulk request form; marketplace for many artisan brands. Trustpilot 3.0. |
| **Zishta** | Ranks for madur mats (₹1,360–1,600) | COD; customisation through "traditional guides"; press-driven blog |
| **Nestasia** | A dedicated handwoven-Sabai collection ranks for "where to buy sabai grass" | Corporate gifts by price band; WhatsApp bulk orders; also sells on Nykaa |
| **indiahandmade.com (Dastkar, Govt)** | #1 for "handmade cane planters india"; corporate gifting listicles | Government authority |
| **The India Craft House** | Sabai product pages with weight, size, origin and "handmade" notes | Free shipping; cashback above ₹2,999; 12-day returns |
| B2B: GlobalLinker, IndiaMART, Karmakriti, Makon Home | Own "sabai grass", "kauna bag" and wedding-basket searches with B2B listings | Bulk and MOQ pricing |

**Open gaps nobody owns:**
- "bulk hamper baskets for diwali": mostly food hamper brands rank.
- "return gift baskets wedding india": no natural-fibre basket maker ranks.

**Our advantage:** prices are lower than the competitors. For example, our madur asana mat is ₹399 against Zishta's ₹1,360 small mat (different products, but the same craft). Our range across Sabai, Kauna, Madurkathi, cane, water hyacinth and bamboo is also wider than any single competitor's.

## 3. Where we lag, and what was done

| # | Gap | Impact | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **94% checkout abandonment** | Highest | Owner: install Microsoft Clarity and check checkout on a phone; turn on Shopify abandoned-checkout emails (Settings → Notifications → Abandoned checkout, send after 1 hour). |
| 2 | **No COD** (competitors offer it) | High | Owner decision: enable COD (Razorpay Magic Checkout, Shiprocket, or Shopify manual "Cash on Delivery"). Charge ₹50 or allow COD only below ₹3,000 to limit returns. |
| 3 | Free shipping at ₹1,300 vs iTokri's ₹500 | Medium | Owner decision. Option: keep ₹1,300 but show "Add ₹X for free shipping" in the cart. |
| 4 | No reviews or stars | High (CTR and trust) | Owner: install Judge.me (free); ask the 21 past buyers for reviews. |
| 5 | Category pages without text | Medium | **Done 17 Oct:** descriptions added to 8 collections. |
| 6 | Weak search titles on the material pages | Medium | **Done:** new SEO titles and descriptions for Sabai, Kash, Madurkathi and Kauna. |
| 7 | No buyer guides for open searches | Medium–High | **Done:** published the madur mat buying guide and the wedding return-gift baskets guide, both with FAQ schema. The Diwali corporate guide was already live (14 Oct). |
| 8 | Image alt text | Low–Medium | **Done:** 356 images. |
