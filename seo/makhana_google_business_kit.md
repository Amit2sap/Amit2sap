# Makhana: Google Business Profile setup kit

Everything below is ready to paste into the new Makhana profile at business.google.com. Facts come from the Shopify product page (`/products/makhana-premium-lotus-seeds-natural-superfood`).

## 1. Profile basics

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Business name** | The real brand name exactly as printed on the pouch, for example "GrassCrafts Makhana". **Don't add keywords** like "best makhana Kolkata"; Google suspends profiles for that. |
| **Primary category** | "Health food store". If you sell mainly by delivery or wholesale rather than from a shop, use "Food products supplier" or "Wholesale food store" instead. |
| **Extra categories** | "Dried fruit store" and "Snack food supplier", whichever of these Google offers. |
| **Website** | `https://grasscrafts.com/collections/makhana?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp&utm_campaign=makhana` |
| **Phone** | The number customers should call or WhatsApp for makhana orders. |
| **Address** | If customers don't visit, **hide the address** and set a **service area** (Kolkata plus the cities you deliver to). Google does not allow a fake shop address. |
| **Hours** | Your real order-taking hours. |
| **Opening date** | The date you started selling makhana. |

## 4. First four posts (one a week; "Updates" post type)

1. **Launch**: "Now on Google: premium Mithila makhana, sun-dried and 100% natural. 250 g / 500 g / 1 kg resealable pouches, delivered across India. Order: grasscrafts.com" + button *Order online*.
2. **Recipe**: "2-minute roasted makhana: 1 tsp ghee, a pinch of rock salt and black pepper, roast on low flame until crisp. Tea-time sorted." + photo of the bowl.
3. **Diwali gifting**: "Gift something wholesome this Diwali: makhana packed in our handwoven grass baskets. Bulk and corporate orders welcome." + button *Learn more* → Diwali collection.
4. **Free shipping**: "Free shipping across India on orders above ₹1,300; the 1 kg pack qualifies on its own." + button *Order online*.

Avoid health or medical claims in posts and ads (for example "for diabetics", "boosts immunity", "cures…"). Google and India's food regulator (FSSAI) restrict them.

## 6. Getting reviews (the biggest ranking factor)

1. In the profile, tap **Ask for reviews** and copy your short review link.
2. Send it to every makhana buyer 3–5 days after delivery:
   > Hi {name}, thank you for ordering GrassCrafts makhana! If you enjoyed it, a quick Google review would really help our small business: {review link}. Thank you — Team GrassCrafts
3. Reply to every review within 48 hours, good or bad.
4. Never offer discounts in exchange for reviews; Google removes them.

Target: 10 reviews in the first month. Once the WhatsApp assistant is live, its review-request message can send this link automatically.

## 7. Verification and connection to the website

- Finish **verification** (video or postcard) before anything shows publicly.
- On grasscrafts.com, link the makhana product page and the footer to the new profile (a "Find us on Google" link). This helps Google trust the profile.
- Keep the name, phone and city **identical** everywhere: Google profile, website, IndiaMART/JustDial listings, and the pouch label.

## 8. Fix on the Shopify product page

The product description says "Low Glycaemic Index — suitable for diabetics" and "supports healthy ageing and immunity". These are health claims that FSSAI regulates and Google Ads may reject. Consider replacing them with neutral wording, for example "Naturally light, no added oil or salt in the pack".
