# GrassCrafts growth plan: Google and AI search

## Baseline (Search Console, 15 Jul – 14 Oct 2026)
- 33,499 impressions, 547 clicks, CTR 1.6%, average position 8.2.
- Clicks come almost only from craft and material searches: "sabai grass" (1,653 impressions, position 7.5), "cane planters" (666, 13.5), "cane basket" (438, 9.6), "kauna grass" (223, 6.3), "madurkathi".
- **Business-buyer searches** (corporate gifts, hamper baskets bulk, jewellery boxes wholesale) bring almost nothing.
- **Mixed-up pages:** for "cane basket", "cane planters", "wall rug" and "cane box", Google shows the **homepage** (position 8–11) instead of the category page (the cane-planter collection sits around position 24).
- **AI search (16 Oct):** GrassCrafts was named in 0 of 7 buyer questions about sabai grass products, kauna baskets, corporate Diwali gifts, jewellery boxes, madurkathi mats, cane planters and bulk hamper baskets. AI answers cite Dharini, Brown Living, Kala Karigari, Kadam Haat, Zishta, Tribes India, Wrap Shap and bioQ instead. It is found only when asked "what is GrassCrafts".
- Structured data: every page and product parses correctly (checked 16 Oct). Google's "Incorrect value type" alert was most likely on a page that has since been removed. Click **Validate fix** in Search Console.

## How AI search engines choose brands (what we act on)
AI answers quote pages that state facts plainly, and brands that **other sites mention**. So we need:
1. **On-site facts:** clear "who, what, where, for whom" pages, FAQs and comparison guides. Done; more each week.
2. **Third-party mentions** (the biggest lever):
   - marketplaces and directories AI already cites (Brown Living, Tribes India, Amazon Karigar, Flipkart Samarth, IndiaMART, JustDial);
   - "best sabai grass / kauna / eco corporate gift brands" listicles and blogs;
   - press coverage of the artisan story;
   - Google Business Profile reviews.
3. **Consistent name:** "GrassCrafts", with the same founder, location and description everywhere.

## Weekly routine (added to the daily schedule)
- **Monday:** check Search Console:
  - track 30 target keywords and the pages that rank for them;
  - list new pages that rank but rarely get clicked;
  - re-run 10 AI-search buyer questions and record whether GrassCrafts is named.
- **Wednesday:** publish one article aimed at buyer questions, linking to the matching collection. Next topics:
  - Bulk hamper baskets for Diwali and corporate gifting: sizes, materials, budgets;
  - Kauna vs Sabai vs cane jewellery boxes, a guide for jewellers;
  - Return-gift baskets for Indian weddings: Haldi, Mehendi, Godh Bharai;
  - Cane planters guide: indoor plants, sizes, care;
  - Madurkathi mats: what they are, uses, how to care for them;
  - Eco-friendly employee onboarding kits in India.
- **Friday:**
  - on-page fixes from Monday's data: titles and descriptions for pages with many impressions but few clicks, and links from blog posts and collections to the right category page so the homepage stops competing with them;
  - one batch of "get cited" emails to listicle authors, bloggers and marketplaces asking them to include GrassCrafts.
