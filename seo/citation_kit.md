## Long description (≈750 characters)
GrassCrafts makes handwoven natural-fibre home decor and gifts with Indian artisans, since 2015. The range includes:
- Sabai grass bags, boxes and wall plates
- Kauna grass hamper baskets and jewellery boxes
- GI-tagged Madurkathi mats from Medinipur
- Sitalpati, cane planters and baskets
- water hyacinth, jute and bamboo pieces

We supply corporate gifts, wedding return-gift baskets, Diwali hampers, planters for hotels and cafés, and stock for décor stores. We also sell plain Mithila makhana. We deliver across India (free shipping on orders of ₹1,300+) and ship internationally. Bulk and custom orders are welcome.

## Makhana description
Plain Mithila makhana (fox nuts / lotus seeds) from Bihar, sun-dried with no oil, salt or preservatives. Sold in 250 g, 500 g and 1 kg packs, with bulk and corporate gift hampers. Delivery across India.
