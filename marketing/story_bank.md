## Verified
- Founded 2015 in Kolkata by Sristi Dey; trade name GrassCrafts by ABALONE design studio, Nimta, Kolkata. (repo: brand facts, Our Story page)
- Over 200 women artisans, spread across different locations. (owner, 29 Oct)
- Work is community-based: different communities make different products. (owner, 29 Oct)
- Skills are passed down generation after generation. (owner, 29 Oct)
- Where a product is made depends on where its raw material comes from. (owner, 29 Oct)
- Madurkathi is a GI-registered craft, GI no. 567. Do not claim a GI for Sabai, Sitalpati or makhana. (repo rule)
- Materials we work with: Sabai, Kauna, Madurkathi, Sitalpati, Kash grass, cane, water hyacinth, bamboo, jute; plus Mithila makhana and handmade dry-flower torans (made to order). (CLAUDE.md)
- Retail facts: free shipping from ₹1,299; no GST on any product; Cash on Delivery with ₹100 extra per COD order. (CLAUDE.md)
- Experience, festive season 2026: a business buyer in Maharashtra found GrassCrafts through ChatGPT search, sent a bulk enquiry through our website, and ordered 12 Nilachal Madurkathi dining sets for festive hampers. Name not for publication without permission. (outreach/won_orders.json)
- Lesson, Oct 2026: we emailed more than 600 businesses in two weeks; the first new bulk order that month came from a buyer who found us through an AI search answer, not from cold email. (sent_log + won_orders; owner may choose whether to share publicly)
- Experience: on our Facebook Lives, viewers ask practical questions: availability of table mats, the price of a red wall hanging, makhana prices, dispatch status. (reports/agents/facebook.md, 20-29 Oct)
- Experience: business buyers this season asked us for price lists, festive volumes and samples, mostly for corporate Diwali and New Year gifting. (outreach/sent_log.json warm leads)
