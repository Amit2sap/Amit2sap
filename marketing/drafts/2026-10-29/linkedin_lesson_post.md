In October we emailed more than 600 businesses about handwoven gift baskets for Diwali and New Year.

Our first new bulk order that month didn't come from any of those emails.

It came from a buyer who asked ChatGPT where to find handwoven dining sets for festive hampers. The answer named GrassCrafts. They filled in the bulk enquiry form on our website, and ordered 12 Madurkathi dining sets.

Three things we are changing because of it:
1. We now write pages that answer the exact questions buyers type into AI assistants, in plain words, with real sizes and prices.
2. We send fewer cold emails, and each one starts with something specific about the business we're writing to.
3. We put our makers' story first: 200+ women artisans, community by community, each working with the grass that grows near them. That is the part no one else can copy.

If you buy gifts for clients or staff: what do you ask an AI assistant before you shortlist a vendor? I'd like to know.
