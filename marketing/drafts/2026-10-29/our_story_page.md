# Our Story (section draft)

**Where a basket is made depends on where its grass grows.**

GrassCrafts started in Kolkata in 2015. Today more than 200 women artisans weave for us, in communities spread across different places. We don't run one big workshop. Each community works with the material that grows around it, and each makes the products it knows best. The skill is not taught in a class: it is passed from one generation to the next.
