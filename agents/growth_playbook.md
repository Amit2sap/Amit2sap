# Growth playbook (owner, 31 Oct 2026). Starts with the first run after the usage reset on Wed 4 Nov 16:30 IST

Goal: grow reach and enquiries from social, search and AI search with real craft content, partner reach and reviews. "Viral" is never promised; agents make more of what the Monday numbers show works and drop what doesn't. This file overrides the cadence lines in instagram.md, pinterest.md, linkedin.md, google_business.md and website.md where they differ. All existing rules still apply: real photos and real video only (image generation paused), no invented facts or numbers, USP wording from `sales/grasscrafts_usp_pitch_2026-10-30.md`, no prices/MOQ/lead times outside the approved catalogue, never comment on other people's posts without owner approval, one job at a time, Sonnet subagents, small outputs.

Baseline (GA4, 28 days to 26 Oct): Organic Social 60 sessions, 0 sales; AI Assistant 91 sessions, 3 orders; Organic Search 213 sessions. Google Business: 2 posts a week, free Upload-Post plan limit of 10 a month.

## 1. Video clips from the owner (the biggest lever)
- Amit or Sristi send 3–5 raw phone clips a week (10–20 s: hands weaving, raw grass to finished basket, PVC bag vs woven basket, packing a hamper, filling a trousseau tray, a bulk order going out with the buyer's permission).
- **Intake:** email the clips to amit2sap@gmail.com with a subject starting `CLIPS`. Each Social run looks for new `CLIPS` mail (Composio Gmail, subject:CLIPS newer_than:7d), logs every clip in `reports/agents/clips_log.md` (date, subject, file, used yes/no), and never reuses a clip within 30 days.
- No clips that week: post photos as normal and add a line to the Monday report "no clips received". Never use stock video or AI video.
- Tooling: if the Composio Instagram connection has no reel or video publish action, log "reel tool missing" once and put the clip and caption on the actions page for the owner to post by hand. Do not work around it.

## 3. Repurpose every article and clip
Each new blog article becomes: 1 Instagram carousel, up to 5 pins (spread over the next week), 1 LinkedIn company post, 1 Google Business post, and a check that `llms.txt` and the AI Search facts still match. Each clip becomes: 1 reel, 1 Facebook reel or post, 1 Pinterest video pin. The Website agent adds the article to `reports/agents/handoff.md` so the Social agent picks it up.

## 4. Collaborations (partner reach), owner approves every one
- **Hamper and gifting brands** already in Pipedrive (Drapednuts, Swagloop, Dhananjay Foods, The Reward Store, Gifting Needs and similar): once a lead replies with interest, propose an Instagram Collab post ("your hamper in our basket"). Draft only; owner approves on the actions page.
- **Micro-creators** (5k–50k followers; home décor, sustainable living, wedding planning, Indian craft): the actions-page kit lists up to 3 a week with their public business email or website, why they fit, and a draft message offering a product for honest content. No follower scraping, no consumer profiling, no paid deals without the owner. Owner approves; product gifting value is the owner's call.
- **Customer photos (UGC):** every review request (`outreach/REVIEW_KIT.md`) also asks permission to share the customer's photo with first name and city. Only reuse photos with written permission, logged in `outreach/reviews_collected.json`.

## 5. Reviews
- Monday Email & Actions run: send review requests by email to buyers whose orders were fulfilled 7–21 days ago and who were not asked before (`outreach/review_requests.json`). WhatsApp review templates only after Meta billing is fixed (agents/TOOLS.md).
- Google Business agent replies to every new review within its run.

## 6. Paid boost (owner decides)
Every Monday the master report names the best-performing reel or post of the week and recommends whether to boost it. Agents never spend money or change ads.

## 7. Measuring (Monday master report, agents/master.md)
Report each week vs the baseline: Organic Social sessions and orders (GA4, Shopify-verified sales only), Instagram reach and saves, Pinterest impressions and outbound clicks, LinkedIn post impressions, Google Business views and website clicks, new Google reviews, collab posts published, clips received and used. Name the best and worst post and the change for next week.
**First 30-day targets (to 4 Dec):** Organic Social 60 → 300+ sessions a month; 1 collab post a week; 10+ new Google reviews; 3 reels a week whenever clips arrive.

## 8. Usage
More posting uses more of the shared weekly limit. Order of priority if the usage guard warns: keep reels and daily pins; drop to photos on posting days only; then pause collab kits. The usage guard's tiers stay as they are.
