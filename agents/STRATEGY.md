## B2B basket-only route and enquiry qualification (owner, 27 Oct 2026, from ChatGPT agent leads)
- **Offer:** basket-only supply for brands that already make gift sets (tea, gourmet, makhana, social-enterprise hampers). Pitch the basket as an optional reusable upgrade to their current packaging, not a replacement of their product.
- **Order of steps:** ask for their real gift-set dimensions → share the verified sample sheet → sample review → only then Sristi prepares a quote. Never invent a price or a production lead time.
- **Qualify every enquiry before production** (reply agent and Sristi use this list): occasion, contents, inner dimensions needed, quantity, target budget per piece, colour, branding (tag, print, ribbon), lining/inserts, delivery city or country, required date, sample needed (paid or free is Sristi's call).
- **International enquiries:** also capture shipping mode, import rules or packaging/material standards (e.g. fumigation, phytosanitary certificate for grass and cane), and landed-cost owner (who pays freight and duty). No physical sample goes abroad until Sristi has checked freight cost and import rules.
- **Priority now:** Tea Trunk and MRK Foods (explicit corporate gifting and customisation programmes), then the 5 queued 26 Oct leads.

## Pipedrive = the one shared lead list (owner decision, 28 Oct 2026)
- Both agent teams (Claude agents and the ChatGPT agent) use Pipedrive; no separate spreadsheets/workbooks for lead status.
- **Pipedrive Leads inbox:** every prospect the ChatGPT agent (or the owner) researches goes in as a Lead with organisation, person, published email/phone, source URL and a note "Source: ChatGPT <date>". Our Email & Actions agent reads open Leads that have a verified email and no outreach yet, emails them under the normal rules (cap, no prices), then adds a note "Emailed <date> by Claude agent".
- **Deals:** every lead that replies, is called by Sristi, gets a catalogue, a quote or an order becomes a Deal (stages Qualified → Contact Made → Demo Scheduled → Proposal Made → Negotiations Started; Won/Lost). Sristi's call outcomes are logged as activities/notes on the deal.
- **Duplicate check before any outreach:** search Pipedrive organisations/persons AND outreach/sent_log.json. If an open Deal exists (Sristi is handling it), do NOT send a cold pitch or follow-up.
- Our outreach/leads/*.json stays as the Claude research store and send log; it is not a second CRM.
