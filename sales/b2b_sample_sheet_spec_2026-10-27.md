# B2B sample sheet: specification (owner, 27 Oct 2026). SRISTI TO SUPPLY DATA

One verified sheet (PDF or Moda page) to attach or link in B2B replies. Every number must be measured; nothing estimated.

Per basket (start with 3 repeatable sizes: small, medium, large):
- Real GrassCrafts photos: full view, weave close-up, inside view, filled example.
- Material (Sabai, Kauna, Kash, cane, water hyacinth), colour (natural beige base), lid or no lid, handle.
- External and internal dimensions (L × W × H, cm and inches), weight.
- What fits (measured): e.g. tea tins, 250 g makhana pack, bottle.
- Indicative single-piece retail price (website price, labelled "retail reference, not a bulk quote").
- Bulk quotation: blank field "quoted on request after quantity and design are confirmed".
- Customisation: colours, sizes, branding, lining, only what the workshop confirms.

Two sections, kept separate: (1) Basket only, (2) Filled hamper.
Not on the sheet: lead times, MOQ, discounts (Sristi quotes these per enquiry).
