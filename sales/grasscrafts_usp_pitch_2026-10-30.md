# GrassCrafts USPs and marketing pitch (owner-approved, 30 Oct 2026)

Every agent (Email & Actions, Lead Research, Social, LinkedIn, Website, ChatGPT agent) uses these USPs and this wording for B2B pitches. Use only the facts here and in CLAUDE.md. Never add prices, discounts, MOQ, lead times or samples (except the Sristi-approved priced catalogue), client names beyond the approved list, certifications or numbers that are not written below.

## The 8 USPs (owner wording)
1. **Made to order, built to your requirements.** Products can be developed around the buyer's requirements, including design, dimensions, shape, colours, materials and functional details, subject to feasibility.
2. **Customisation for your brand.** Tailored baskets, gift hampers, reusable packaging and product presentation to match your brand identity and occasion.
3. **Authentic Indian artisan craftsmanship.** Handcrafted products showcasing traditional weaving skills and distinctive natural textures.
4. **Natural-fibre alternatives.** Sabai grass, Kauna grass, Madurkathi, cane, bamboo, jute and other natural materials as distinctive alternatives to conventional synthetic products.
5. **Premium, differentiated presentation.** Unique handcrafted designs for corporate gifts, weddings, luxury products and premium retail.
6. **Reusable products with everyday utility.** Many baskets, containers and home accessories keep serving a purpose after gifting.
7. **Versatile B2B solutions.** A broad product range for corporate gifting, hospitality, wedding hampers, retail packaging and home décor.
8. **Artisan-made products with a story.** Products that help businesses communicate craftsmanship, cultural heritage and thoughtful material choices.

Supporting fact you may use (owner confirmed 29 Oct): GrassCrafts works with over 200 women artisans across different locations; different communities make different products, with skills passed down through generations, and where a product is made depends on where its raw material comes from. Never add wages, percentages or other numbers.

## One-liner
GrassCrafts makes handwoven natural-fibre baskets, hampers and packaging, made to order and customised for your brand by Indian artisan communities.

## Short pitch (about 60 words; LinkedIn, Instagram bio, WhatsApp follow-up after a reply)

We build to your brief: design, size, shape, colour and finish, subject to feasibility. ## Do not say
- "Living wages", wage premiums, percentages, artisan earnings, carbon or tree numbers, "fair trade", certifications or GI tags we don't hold (only Madurkathi is GI-tagged).
- "Plastic-free" for a whole order unless every component is natural; prefer "natural-fibre alternative to synthetic packaging".
- Any promise of delivery dates, minimum quantities or free samples.
