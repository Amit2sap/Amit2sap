**How to read this.**
- Everything comes from public sources, and every row has a source link.
- Anything marked "estimate:" is our inference, not a fact.

## 1. Business buyers already buying our category

### Corporate gifting (31 buyers, mostly from gifting firms' own case studies)
**Who they buy from:** BoxUp (19 case studies), The Knot Company, Ferns N Petals, Ace Gifting, Bombay Sweet Shop and Giftana. Brown Living, Nestasia and iTokri do not name their buyers; Brown Living only says "300+ organisations".

**Published quantities:**

| Buyer | Quantity | Supplier |
|---|---|---|
| Reliance Jewels | 8,000 gift baskets | FNP |
| JK Lakshmi Cement | 3,000 gift baskets and bouquets | FNP |
| Accenture | 3,000 | — |
| Aadvi Group | 200 | — |
| Toshiba | 200 | — |

Bombay Sweet Shop's minimum for custom orders is 50.

**Closest match to us:** Techcanopy Software Labs, Bengaluru. Its Diwali employee hampers were built on baskets handwoven by Karigara artisans, through The House of Krinay.

**What is inside a typical hamper:**
- jute pouches of dry fruit;
- brass, wooden or terracotta diyas;
- a box branded with the client's logo.

Where an open presentation was wanted (Zomato, Flipkart, Maa Homes), BoxUp used faux-leather baskets. Kauna or Sabai can replace these.

**What these clients ask for:**
- Branding on every item.
- Cisco/NetApp and Travel XP asked for eco gifts with no food.
- The Knot Company uses 13×10×4 in water-hyacinth U-baskets.

**Occasions:** mostly Diwali, for both clients and employees. Also onboarding kits, dealer meets, launches, Women's Day, Mother's Day and home handovers.

**Gifting firms that use natural-fibre baskets themselves.** These are buyers *for* us:
- Kavyatva (river grass and palm leaf)
- Kottanz (palm-leaf hamper, ₹550)
- FNP Corporate (round Sabai hamper, ₹1,500)
- Swageazy (800+ enterprise clients, new funding)

- Jewellers' gift-with-purchase is gold coins (CaratLane, Jewelbox), not baskets.
- Mia by Tanishq uses paper boxes with seed cards.
- We should pitch display trays and bridal boxes instead.
- Watch-packaging suppliers sell only board, MDF and leatherette boxes, so **a natural-fibre box is unclaimed**.

### Colours
Named bright colours sell for Sabai. "Natural" leads for Kauna and water hyacinth.

### Occasions and timing
| Occasion | When |
|---|---|
| Rakhi | Jul–Aug |
| Diwali | searches rise from July and peak in October |
| Return gifts | surge in late November, with the wedding season |
| Weddings | Nov–Feb; trousseau booked 3–4 months ahead |

Competitors' return gifts are candles and brass, not grass baskets.

### What reviewers say
- **Praise:** eco-friendly, sturdy yet light, beautiful as a gift.
- **Complaints:** grassy or chemical smell, smaller than pictured, colour and shape variation, loose ends.

### Makhana
It is a mass seller on Amazon: leading 100–200 g packs sell 20,000+ a month.

   - basket-only supply to gifting firms, hamper studios and planners (MOQ 25–50, matching the market);
   - room amenity sets for hotels (laundry basket, tissue-box cover, tray);
   - a return-gift tier (₹150–500 market band, optional makhana fill).
2. **Register as a GeM seller.** Cane and bamboo baskets come up again and again.
3. **Reply to the go4WorldBusiness requests**: the Netherlands baskets, the Hong Kong mats (offer Madurkathi) and the UAE makhana. Check export documents first.

**For us (no owner sign-off needed):**
1. **Product pages:**

   - "wedding hamper basket" and "bulk hamper baskets";

## Gaps
- **Login walls:** IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Alibaba RFQ, Amazon Business and Udaan buy requests need a login, and Volza import data is blocked.
- **Missing details:** published quantities and supplier names are rare, and buyer emails are almost never published.
- **Checking contacts:** the 109 new leads have published business contacts. Recheck the few flagged in their notes before sending.
