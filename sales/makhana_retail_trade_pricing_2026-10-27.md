# Makhana for offline grocery stores: trade price proposal (27 Oct 2026) — OWNER/SRISTI TO CONFIRM

Benchmarks (web, general FMCG, not makhana-specific): retailer margin ~15% on staples up to 25–30% for snacks/new brands; distributor 5–12%. Kirana blended gross margin high single digits to mid teens. Sources: accountune.com/kirana-store-profit-margin, echai.ventures general-trade margins, business-standard.com distributor margins.

Rule: shelf MRP = website price (never undercut our own site). Offer the store 20% off MRP (25% for a first order / launch). Price to retailer (PTR) below.

| Pack | MRP | PTR at 20% | PTR at 25% (launch) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100 g | ₹199 | ₹159 | ₹149 |
| 250 g | ₹499 | ₹399 | ₹374 |
| 500 g | ₹849 | ₹679 | ₹637 |
| 1 kg | ₹1,599 | ₹1,279 | ₹1,199 |

- Via a distributor: add 8–10% more off MRP for the distributor (store still gets 20%).
- Check cost: our costs are assumed (50% of retail, sales/makhana_draft_pricing_2026-10-25.md). At 25% off we keep 75% of MRP; confirm real cost + delivery before offering.
- Before shelf sale: packaged food must carry the FSSAI licence number, MRP, net weight, packing and best-before dates (Legal Metrology); confirm GST treatment for B2B invoices with the accountant.
- Start: 100 g and 250 g only (impulse price points), 6–12 packs per store, top-up on sell-out.
