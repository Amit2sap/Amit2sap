# Makhana draft products: estimated prices (25 Oct 2026, owner request; SRISTI TO REVIEW)

No real cost prices exist in Shopify or in this repo. Every cost below is an ASSUMPTION, so the prices are only a starting point. Sristi: replace the cost lines with real numbers and re-run the formula.

**Formula:** price = total cost ÷ 0.8 (20% profit margin on the selling price), then rounded up to a ₹x49 / ₹x99 price.
**Cost assumption:** item cost = 50% of GrassCrafts' current retail price for that item (makhana 250 g ₹499, 500 g ₹849, 1 kg ₹1,599; Sabai lidded basket with wooden handle S ₹699, M ₹850).
**Delivery:** included only where the buyer gets free shipping (orders ₹1,299+, and makhana-only orders, which ship free). Below ₹1,299 the buyer already pays ₹120 shipping, so delivery is left out.
