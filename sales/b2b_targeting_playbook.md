## 3. Buyer personas

**A. "Priya", HR / Employee Engagement Manager** (IT or BFSI, 500–5,000 staff)
- Needs: 6–8 gifting moments a year, an approved vendor, GST invoice, delivery to multiple offices, branding.
- Worries: late delivery, breakage, quality varying between units, approval cycles.
- Hooks: "one vendor for every occasion", sustainability and ESG story (plastic-free, artisan livelihoods), sample kit, a yearly gifting calendar from us.
- Ask: a 15-minute call, then a sample box, then a trial order, then an annual rate contract.

**B. "Rohit", Founder / Sourcing Head, corporate gifting company**
- Needs: margin (30–40%), reliable bulk supply, custom sizes, fast turnaround in October and November.
- Hooks: wholesale price list, lead times, MOQ, white-label option.
- Ask: put GrassCrafts in their catalogue.

**C. "Mr Mehta", Purchase Manager, hotel or resort**
- Needs: durable, natural amenity baskets and trays, regular replenishment, vendor registration.
- Hooks: room and bathroom amenity trays, bread baskets, spa trays, lobby festive decor, guest welcome hampers.
- Ask: a vendor registration form, then a sample set.

**D. "Neha", Founder / Packaging Head, D2C food or tea brand**
- Needs: premium reusable packaging that lifts perceived value; custom sizes for their SKUs.
- Hooks: "box becomes the gift", unboxing photos, plastic-free claims for marketing.
- Ask: samples sized to their product.

## 4. How the daily outreach uses this

1. **Each day's 150 emails** are split roughly 30% corporate gifting firms, 25% D2C food / sweets / tea brands, 15% hotels and resorts, 15% wedding planners, 15% jewellers and decor stores.
2. **Named decision-makers**: up to 20 personal emails a day to the roles in section 1. Find them with Hunter.io: the free plan has 50 domain searches a month, about 30 left this cycle. Otherwise find the name on the company website or in press coverage, guess the address from the company's email pattern, and check that the mailbox exists. One personal email each; respect "no thanks".
3. **Follow-up rule**: one polite follow-up 5 days after the first email if there's no reply, then a second 7 days later with a sample offer. After that, stop.
4. **Seasonal angle**: the email names the next occasion on the calendar above. In Oct–Nov that's Diwali and the wedding season; in Dec it's New Year and client gifting.

## 5. Needed from the owner

- Minimum order and wholesale discount (for gifting companies).
- Whether logo branding is possible (printed tags, stamped boxes).
- Real lead times for 100 / 500 / 1,000 units.
- GST number on invoices (corporates ask for it).
