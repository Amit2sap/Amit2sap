# Facebook Messenger: buyer insights (15 Oct 2026)

Source: all 25 Messenger threads on the "Grasscrafts by Abalone" Page (June–October 2026). Customer names are left out on purpose.

## What buyers asked

| Intent | Threads | Typical message |
|---|---|---|
| **Price of a product they saw** | 5 | Photo + "Pp" (price please), "How much is it?", "What will be the price of this. Is this kauna bag" |
| Clicked an ad and tapped the preset questions | 15 | "Premium wedding/housewarming gift options?", "Custom size or finish?", "Lead time?", "Artisans and materials?" |
| Manufacturer / sourcing question (possible reseller) | 1 | "R u manufacturing it" |
| Greeting only | 3 | "Hello mam", "🙏" |

**No thread ever got a reply from a person.** Every one got only an automatic message.

## Buyer types

1. **Price-checker with a product in mind** (highest intent). Sends a screenshot or names a product (Kauna bag). Wants price, size and how to order in one message. Lost today because the auto-reply only says "WhatsApp us".
2. **Gift seeker** (wedding, housewarming, now Diwali). Wants 3–4 options by budget, gift wrapping and delivery date.
3. **Custom/bulk enquirer.** Asks about custom size or finish, or whether we manufacture. Could be an interior designer, a reseller or a corporate buyer. Needs a person to call back.
4. **Ad tapper** (low intent). Taps every preset button, never types. Mostly curiosity, so it's better to send them to the website than to spend time on them.

## Problems with the current automatic replies

- The Page auto-reply sends people to WhatsApp, an old Facebook page and a cut-off "Our instagram handle" line, with no price and no product link.
- The ad's instant replies still say **"Mother's Day"** and fire twice per question.
- The wording ("your estate", "Heritage Storage Trunks") doesn't match what buyers ask, and gives no prices or links.

## Fixes

1. Replace the Page instant reply with the text below (Business Suite → Inbox → Automations → Instant reply).
2. Edit the ad's preset answers so each one has a price range and a link. Remove "Mother's Day".
3. Answer a "Pp" or photo enquiry with the price within 1 hour. Messenger allows replies for 24 hours after a customer's message (7 days for a human reply).
