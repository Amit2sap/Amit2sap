## Coexistence: what changes in the WhatsApp Business app (Meta documentation)
- Needs Business app v2.24.17 or later and a number that has been active for a while (sources say 7–30 days). The move is one way: app to API.
- Syncs individual chats from the last 6 months, plus contacts. Group chats do not sync.
- **Broadcast lists are disabled.** Existing lists become read-only and no new ones can be made, so broadcasts have to go through the partner, using approved marketing templates sent to opted-in contacts.
- Disappearing and view-once messages and live location are switched off. Calls, catalogue, status, labels and quick replies keep working in the app.
- Throughput is capped at 20 messages a second, which is plenty for GrassCrafts.
