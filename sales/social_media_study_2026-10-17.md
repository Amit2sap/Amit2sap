## 2. Facebook: what works and what doesn't (last 50 posts, 11 Jul – 14 Oct)

| Post type | Typical reactions / comments / shares |
|---|---|
| **Live sale videos** (Diwali, Navratri, "everything under ₹500") | **15–43 / 50–88 / about 205–224** |
| "We go live at 2:30 PM" announcement posts | 0–5 / 0 / 0–1 |
| Product photo posts (new arrivals, cane planter, hampers) | 0–4 / 0–1 / 0 |
| Bare links to Instagram reels | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| Posts with no caption | 0–6 / 0 / 0 |

**Takeaways:**
1. **Live selling is the brand's real sales engine on Facebook.** Each Live gets more than 50 comments, and those comments are orders and price questions that move to WhatsApp. These sales mostly don't pass through the Shopify checkout, which is one reason Shopify shows so few orders.
2. Every Live gets almost exactly 205–224 shares. That looks like regular sharing into groups, which is a good habit worth keeping.
3. Everything else gets almost no reach. Posts are often duplicated (the same text 2–3 times a day), have no caption, or are just reel links. All three formats perform badly on Facebook.
4. **There is no system to capture Live buyers.** Viewers are not tagged, sent a catalogue link, or followed up with a Shopify cart or checkout link.

## 4. Strategy: what to change (by revenue impact)

1. **Turn Lives into tracked sales.**
   - Before each Live, post one teaser only, not three.
   - During the Live, pin the comment "Comment the product number. WhatsApp 6290744782 or buy at grasscrafts.com/collections/diwali-gift-hampers".
   - After the Live, post the same day "Everything from today's Live", linking to a Shopify collection of the items shown.
   - Log every Live order in Shopify (draft order, then send the invoice link) so we can measure it and so buyers get order and shipping emails.
2. **Link Instagram to the Facebook Page as a Business account** (Meta Business Suite → Settings → Instagram accounts). This unlocks:
   - one inbox for both;
   - Instagram Shopping tags from the Shopify catalogue;
   - insights we can analyse (through Composio or Windsor.ai).
3. **Use one brand name everywhere.**
   - Rename "Grasscrafts by Abalone" to "GrassCrafts" and set the vanity URL facebook.com/grasscrafts (if available).
   - Delete or merge the two empty pages.
4. **Set up a Google Business Profile** (category Handicraft or Home goods store) and collect reviews. Reviews feed both Google and AI answers.
5. **Create a LinkedIn company page.**
   - Sristi posts weekly about artisans, corporate gifting and the founder story.
   - Amit posts B2B updates.
   - This supports the B2B email outreach, since decision-makers check LinkedIn.
6. **Weekly content mix on Instagram and Facebook** (one post a day, no duplicates):
   - **Mon:** artisan or process reel ("from Loktak wetland to hamper basket").
   - **Tue:** product with price and link.
   - **Wed:** blog guide carousel (madur mat guide, wedding return gifts).
   - **Thu:** customer photo or review (from the review replies now coming in).
   - **Fri:** corporate or wedding bulk showcase.
   - **Sat or Sun:** Live sale.
7. **Pinterest business account.** Pin every product and blog guide; home decor buyers use Pinterest to plan, and it already sends traffic.
8. **Reseller and creator programme**, like iTokri's Craft Partner. Give Kolkata boutiques and micro-creators a code and a commission, and offer them Live co-hosting.
