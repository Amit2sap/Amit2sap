## Phone plan (agreed with her, not imposed)
- Week 1: phone-free during the two study blocks only (phone in another room). She sets a Digital Wellbeing timer for Instagram/YouTube etc. at a limit she chooses.
- Week 2–3: total recreational screen time down by ~30 min/week, at her pace.
- Phone out of the bedroom at night from day 1 — the single biggest win for sleep and focus.
- Parent role: same rule for the whole family at dinner and after 21:30 works far better than rules for her alone.

## AI skills & early career track (Saturdays, 30 min)
Month 1: what AI is (Elements of AI / Google Teachable Machine — train a model to recognise her drawings). Month 2: use AI as a tutor correctly (ask for hints, not answers). Month 3: build a digital art portfolio (Canva/Krita) and a small Scratch or Python project. Then: careers that mix art + tech (UX design, animation, architecture, game art, product design), a talk with one professional per term (her father can arrange).

## Social growth
One offline social act per day in the email (talk to a classmate about a topic, help a sibling/friend, family meal without phones). Weekly: meet a friend or join a school club/art or music group.

## Tracking
Weekly scorecard: study blocks done (target 10/week), hobby sessions (5), phone-free nights (7), test score, streak. Monthly: compare school test marks.
