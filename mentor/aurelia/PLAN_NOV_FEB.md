# Aurelia — study plan Nov 2026 to Feb 2027 (Class 9 finals)

Set by the coach on 30 Oct 2026 at her father's direction: **Maths and Science come first.** Her own ideas are welcome and used where they are practical (see "Her plan, reviewed"). The coach is her teacher and mentor here: it explains every decision and keeps it kind, but it does not drop Maths and Science.

## Why Maths and Science first (tell her this, simply)
1. They carry the most marks and build on themselves: Class 10 Maths, Physics and Chemistry start from Class 9 chapters.
2. Leaving the hardest subjects to January means meeting them in the most stressful month, with no time to fix gaps. Little and often beats a big push at the end (spaced practice: what you revise across weeks stays; what you cram fades).
3. Her maths tuition (Tue/Thu) already runs now; practising alongside it doubles its value.
4. Hard subjects go in her freshest slot. Favourite subjects come after, as the "reward" block, which keeps her motivated.

## How to teach, not just tell
- Maths: 5 problems a day, show working; the coach checks steps she sends and explains mistakes.
- Science: explain it back in 3 lines (teach-back), draw the diagram, then 3 questions.
- Error log: every wrong answer goes in a notebook with the right method; revisited each Sunday.
- Link to her loves (JOY.md): baking = Chemistry, dance = Physics.

## How the coach handles her suggestions from now on
- Listen first, thank her, and use what is practical.
- If a suggestion would hurt her results (dropping a subject, leaving Maths/Science to the end, cramming), explain kindly in 2–3 lines why it won't work, offer the closest workable option, and keep Maths and Science first. Never sarcastic or harsh; never blame.
- She chooses within the plan: which chapter first, which order inside a day, which reward, which subject in "her choice" slots.
