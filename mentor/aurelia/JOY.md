# Joy, stories and fun tasks (father, 29 Oct 2026)

Goal: she enjoys life again. Every email carries ONE item from this file, besides study. Rotate; never repeat the same item within 2 weeks. Keep it light, never preachy. Log what was used in the daily log.

## Her loves (use these first)
Dance (her own pick, firm) · cake making and baking · swimming · drawing · guitar · art and crafts.

## 1. Story of the day (PLAN email, 4–6 lines)
- Real, verified stories only (check facts with a quick search; name the person; no invented quotes). Prefer young Indian achievers, women, and people who came back from a slump.
- Mix: dancers and choreographers; bakers and chefs who started at home; swimmers; artists and musicians; scientists and founders who started as teens; everyday kindness stories.
- End with one question for her ("What would you have done?").
- Fiction is allowed only if clearly marked "a short story" (e.g. a 6-line mystery she finishes in her reply).

## 2. Joy task (one a day, 10–30 min, offline)
Weekdays (pick one):
- **Dance break:** 10 minutes to one favourite song, phone in another room after starting the music (or speaker). Once a week: learn 8 counts of a new routine.
- **Bake mini-task:** read one recipe and write its ingredients as ratios; plan Saturday's bake; decorate a plain biscuit.
- Draw/doodle 15 min; guitar 20 min; a 5-minute craft from things at home.
- Kindness: do one nice thing for someone at home without being asked.
Weekends:
- **Saturday bake project** (with an adult present for the oven/knives): one recipe a week, e.g. mug cake → banana bread → cupcakes → eggless sponge → decorated cake. She photographs the result (food only, no faces) and may send it.
- **Sunday dance:** 30 min learning a routine, or dance with family.
- Swim, art project, meet a friend.

## 3. Link joy to study (once a day in PLAN, one line)
- Baking = Chemistry (baking soda + acid → carbon dioxide; why cakes rise), Biology (yeast is alive), Maths (ratios, scaling a recipe for 6 people, unit conversion), Physics (heat transfer in an oven).
- Dance = Physics (balance, centre of gravity, momentum, rhythm and frequency), Biology (muscles, heart rate), Maths (counting in 8s, patterns).
- Swimming = Physics (buoyancy, Archimedes' principle, drag). Guitar = Physics (sound, frequency, string length). Art = Maths (symmetry, perspective).

## 4. Fun fact (NIGHT email, one line)
Surprising, true, short: science, food, music, space, animals, India.

## 5. Dream jobs from what she loves (Saturday, once a fortnight)
Choreographer, dance teacher, dance physiotherapist; pastry chef, food scientist, bakery founder, food photographer/stylist; sports scientist; illustrator, animator, game artist; music producer. What the job is, the skills, one real person doing it, one tiny step she could take now.

## 6. Small rewards she chooses
After a 5-day streak: she picks a treat (bake something new, a dance class, an art supply, a family outing). Suggest it to her father in the report; the coach never promises money or gifts itself.

## Safety
Oven, knives and stove only with an adult nearby. Food photos only, never faces. Dance videos stay private (not posted online) unless her parents agree.
