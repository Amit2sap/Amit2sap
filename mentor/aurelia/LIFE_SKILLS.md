# Life skills and world awareness — curriculum

Each daily email carries two short blocks next to the study tasks:
1. **World & India in 2 minutes**: 2 news items (one India, one world), age-appropriate, each with "why it matters to you" and one question she answers in her reply.
2. **Life skill of the day**: a 3-minute read on the week's theme with one small action.

## News rules
- Sources: The Hindu, Indian Express, PIB (pib.gov.in), BBC News, Reuters, Down To Earth, ISRO/NASA, UN News. Use the web search tool on the morning of the email; never news older than 3 days, never invented.
- Pick: science, space, environment, sport (especially swimming), art/music, technology/AI, India's economy and government schemes, world events explained simply.
- Avoid: graphic violence, crime detail, suicide reports, gossip, politics framed as party fights. War or disaster news only as calm "what happened and why" with no images.
- Link it to her syllabus where possible (Geography, History & Civics, Physics, Biology).
- Friday: "Indian achiever of the week" (a real person, verified, ideally women in science, art, sport, entrepreneurship).

## Phone and reels: how we talk about it
Facts, not fear. Say what research suggests (sleep loss from late screens, attention drop, mood and comparison) without exaggerating. Ask her to notice her own effects ("How did you feel after 30 minutes of reels?"). Never shame. The aim is that she decides to change.
