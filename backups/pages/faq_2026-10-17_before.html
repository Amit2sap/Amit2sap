About Our Products

What types of products does Grasscrafts offer?

Grasscrafts offers handwoven home decor, storage baskets and organizers, bags, kitchen decor, corporate gifts, festive gifts, and bridal decorative items — all crafted from natural grass fibers by Indian artisans.

What materials are used in Grasscrafts products?

We use 100% natural and sustainable materials including Sitalpati, kauna, madurkathi, jute, bamboo, cane, and seagrass. Each material is chosen for its durability, flexibility, and low environmental impact.

Are Sitalpati and kauna grass products durable?

Yes. Sitalpati and kauna grass are naturally strong, moisture-resistant, and long-lasting when cared for properly. They are ideal for baskets, mats, and storage solutions used daily.

Where are Grasscrafts products made?

All our products are handcrafted by skilled artisans across India — particularly from regions in West Bengal where Sitalpati and madurkathi weaving traditions have been practiced for generations.

Is customization available?

Yes! We offer customization in design, size, and material. Contact us with your requirements and our artisans will create a bespoke piece for you. Ideal for corporate gifting and bulk orders.

Sustainability & Ethics

Are Grasscrafts products eco-friendly?

Absolutely. All our products are made from renewable natural fibers with no synthetic materials. Our production supports traditional artisan communities and promotes zero-waste, slow-making practices.

Are natural grass fiber products biodegradable?

Yes — Sitalpati, kauna, jute, and seagrass are fully biodegradable and compostable at end of life, making them a genuinely sustainable alternative to plastic storage and decor.

How does buying from Grasscrafts support artisans?

Every purchase directly supports Indian artisan families who depend on traditional weaving for their livelihood. We work with local communities to ensure fair wages and preservation of centuries-old craft techniques.

Care & Maintenance

How do I care for natural grass fiber products?

Keep away from prolonged moisture and direct sunlight. Wipe with a dry or slightly damp cloth. Store in a cool, dry place. Avoid machine washing. With proper care, these products last for years.

Can grass baskets be used outdoors?

Our baskets are best suited for indoor use. Occasional outdoor use in shaded, dry conditions is fine, but prolonged exposure to rain or direct sun may reduce their lifespan.
