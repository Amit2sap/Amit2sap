GrassCrafts is an Indian manufacturer of handwoven natural-fibre gifts and packaging, founded in 2015 by designer Sristi Dey. We make hamper baskets, trays, gift boxes, jewellery boxes, planters and decor from Kauna grass, Sabai grass, Kash grass, Madurkathi, cane, bamboo, jute and water hyacinth, woven by artisan communities across India, mainly in West Bengal. We supply businesses in bulk, with custom sizes and colours, and deliver across India.
